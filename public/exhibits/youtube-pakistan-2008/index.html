YouTube, Briefly Owned by Pakistan Telecom

2008-02-24 · about 2 hours

Impact 2.8/10

  1. 1Pakistan Telecom is ordered to block YouTube domestically
  2. 2It announces a more specific route for YouTube's address block to do so
  3. 3Its upstream provider passes the announcement on to the rest of the internet
  4. 4Routers everywhere prefer the more specific route
  5. 5Most of the world's YouTube traffic heads into Pakistan and disappears ✕

BGP believes what it is told. Filter what your customers announce.

Source: RIPE NCC case study, 2008
